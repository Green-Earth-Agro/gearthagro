# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Primary: international buyers, B2B partners, and investors evaluating GreenEarth Agro as a sourcing partner or investment. They need to quickly establish credibility, scale, and supply reliability.

Secondary: smallholder farmers and local agri-business contacts in Ghana who may engage as supply-side partners.

Tertiary: general public, press, and NGOs interested in sustainable agro-processing in West Africa.

## Product Purpose

GreenEarth Agro is a Ghana-based agro-processing company that integrates four business streams: own farming operations, smallholder farmer sourcing, value-added processing, and biomass utilization. The website is the company's official public face, communicating scale, capability, and trustworthiness to international buyers and partners while establishing GreenEarth Agro as a modern, credible agro-industry leader in West Africa.

## Brand Personality

Innovative, modern, progressive. The brand should feel like a serious, globally-minded company rooted in Ghana, not a generic agri-corp or a startup. It projects confidence without arrogance, technical capability without sterility, and local pride without folksy regionalism. Voice is direct and evidence-led.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS / startup cream: white backgrounds, Inter font, pale blue-green gradients, floating blob shapes, hero CTAs that feel like a software product.
- Heavy GIS / enterprise geo tools: cluttered toolbar-heavy interfaces, dark map-heavy layouts.
- Farm-kitsch: barn illustrations, rustic wood textures, tractor icons, "down-home" aesthetic that undermines the company's industrial scale.
- Dark mode data-wall: dense dark dashboards covered in numbers, no breathing room.

## Design Principles

1. **Prove it, don't claim it.** Use specifics (tonnage, farmer count, regions served, processing capacity) instead of adjectives like "leading" or "trusted." Every hero claim needs a supporting fact nearby.
2. **Global quality, Ghanaian roots.** The brand competes internationally but its identity is inseparable from West African agriculture. Surface this through imagery, language, and visual references to the land, not clichés.
3. **The supply chain is the story.** The four-stream model (own farm + smallholder + processing + biomass) is the competitive differentiator. Make it legible and prominent, not buried in a company overview paragraph.
4. **Earn trust through structure.** B2B buyers and investors assess credibility from how organized a company looks, not just what it says. Clean hierarchy, deliberate pacing, and no visual noise communicate operational competence.
5. **Progressive without sterile.** "Innovative and modern" means scientific confidence and forward momentum, not cold minimalism. Warmth from the landscape and people coexists with technical precision.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Sufficient contrast on all text. Keyboard navigable. Screen reader compatible landmarks and alt text. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
